# Ilustraciones provisionales del tablero

Archivo: [concept-atlas.png](concept-atlas.png). Creado el 2026-10-27 con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes, en modo generación nueva, sin imagen de entrada. No se ha usado CLI/API ni se ha extraído arte del PDF. Destino en el proyecto: `assets/images/board/concept-atlas.png`.

Es un atlas de cuatro ilustraciones conceptuales; `board_style.gd` usa AtlasTexture para mostrar cada cuadrante sin modificar la imagen original. Es arte provisional para revisar P05, no material oficial ni ilustraciones aprobadas para el juego final.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one concept-art atlas for prototype cards of a Mistborn-inspired private digital board game. Create a SINGLE square image divided exactly into FOUR equally sized square quadrants in a 2x2 arrangement. No gaps, no borders, no lettering, no numbers, no UI, no card frames, no watermark. Each quadrant is an independent polished painterly fantasy illustration, cinematic atmosphere, fine brush texture, restrained palette of deep midnight navy, charcoal, cool silver mist, warm ivory highlights, weathered brass and copper. Top left: a young dark-haired woman in a dark blue flowing mistcloak standing on a misty gothic rooftop, three small coins hovering near one gloved hand, silver moonlit sky. Top right: a blond male rebel in a charcoal cloak, half-length portrait, noble but worn expression, rooftop city in copper dusk behind him. Bottom left: close still life of a handful of ancient gold and copper coins scattered on black slate, subtle cool reflected blue light, elegant tactile metal. Bottom right: a wide atmospheric old gothic city with towers, canals, distant spires, rolling blue-silver mist and a small warm ivory dusk glow on the horizon; no characters. Full bleed compositions in all four panels; attractive original concept artwork. Keep all art clean, composed and legible at small card sizes. No modern objects, no neon, no photorealistic collage.
```

La paleta y procedencia están en [dirección visual](../../../docs/diseno-visual.md). La generación solo crea ilustraciones; todos los textos, controles, marcos y estados son controles nativos de Godot.
